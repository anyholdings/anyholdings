About Us

Software Ventures, Structured for Growth

Armitage & York Holdings LLC is a Series Limited Liability Company organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming. Founded in 2019, the company serves as a holding entity for software technology ventures, providing centralized governance, shared infrastructure, and liability isolation across its portfolio of operating companies.

Each series operates as an independent business unit with its own assets, liabilities, and intellectual property, while benefiting from the resources and oversight of the parent organization.
