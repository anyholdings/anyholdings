About

Centralized Infrastructure, Independent Operations

Exactio Labs is the technology operations arm of Armitage & York Holdings LLC. We provide the shared backbone — cloud infrastructure, development tooling, security, and DevOps — that enables each portfolio company to focus on building great products without duplicating foundational resources.

By consolidating core infrastructure under a single operational brand, we ensure consistency, reduce overhead, and maintain enterprise-grade standards across every series in the portfolio.
